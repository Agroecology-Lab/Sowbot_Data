MeshCore avoid: sweep and replay

Simulation only, never run on hardware, not a safety function. One collision domain, and a lost frame is lost for every receiver at once. No range, no tracking error.

Sweep:

Seeds in the selected cell

Red: two moving robots came closer than the clearance. Amber: did not finish. Green: finished and kept clear.

Replay

Each ring has radius half the clearance, so two touching rings are one clearance apart. Filled: driving. Hollow: waiting or at a side-step stop. Grey: finished. Expanding ring: frame sent. Dashed red: frame lost.
